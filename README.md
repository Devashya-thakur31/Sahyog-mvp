# SAHYOG

SAHYOG is a platform being built to connect donors, volunteers, orphanages, old-age homes, NGOs, and other social organizations on a single platform.

The idea started with a simple question:

How can people help organizations in need while knowing where their contribution is going and what impact it creates?

SAHYOG aims to make that process easier, more transparent, and more accessible.

## What the platform will do

### For Donors

- Discover verified organizations
- Support specific needs and causes
- Track donations and impact updates
- Save organizations for future support
- Communicate with organizations

### For Volunteers

- Find volunteering opportunities
- Apply directly through the platform
- Track applications and participation history
- Receive certificates for completed activities

### For Organizations

- Create and manage needs
- Receive donations
- Manage volunteer opportunities
- Share impact updates with supporters
- Connect with donors and volunteers

### For Administrators

- Verify organizations
- Manage users and content
- Review platform activity
- Maintain trust and transparency across the platform

## Vision

The long-term goal of SAHYOG is to create a platform where helping others is simple, transparent, and measurable.

Starting from Madhya Pradesh, the platform is designed with the possibility of expanding to more regions in the future.
